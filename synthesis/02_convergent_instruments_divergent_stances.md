## Thesis (SETTLED by essay 05 — Atomic Blonde, the designated thesis-breaker, held)
The two shows perform **one feminist analysis through two rhetorical economies.** Where their bottom-line verdicts diverge, the divergence traces to **what their instruments are built to measure**, not to disagreement about the film (Atomic Blonde: SCUM 28 ≈ joint-worst vs BC 3 nipples, from a shot-for-shot convergent reading — SCUM weights smarm, the nipple scale weights representation-progress). The shows differ far less in conclusions than in rhetorical economy. Both hold scoring instruments (SCUM/Kronsteen/Kaufman/GIRTH vs the Bechdel test + nipple scale); both reach the same verdicts about the same films. What differs is **stance toward the instrument** and the **affective grammar** of enforcement — not the analysis.
