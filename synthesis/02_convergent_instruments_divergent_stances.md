## Thesis (provisional, on probation until essay 05)
The shows differ **far less in conclusions than in rhetorical economy.** Both hold scoring instruments (SCUM/Kronsteen/Kaufman/GIRTH vs the Bechdel test + nipple scale); both reach the same verdicts about the same films. What differs is **stance toward the instrument** and the **affective grammar** of enforcement — not the analysis.

## Stance & instrument (H8)
- Instruments are **elastic** for both shows (SCUM misogyny 8/7 above ceiling; BC's "concave nipple… a negative one" below floor — essay 03). Stance is the whole difference: KJB performs belief-as-bit while obeying; BC believes-as-pedagogy with developing case law.
- Award direction (H10): KJB decorates the disposable/marginal + formalises desire into a trophy (Kaufman Star); BC withholds + gifts to admired women, runs an anti-horniness platform. Approach-grammar awards arousal; avoidance-grammar abolishes it.

## The affect-grammar inversion (H16) — the theoretical payload
Both shows are egalitarian-left, but enforce through opposite poles of the Schröder approach/avoidance axis: BC purity-disgust (the pole the literature codes authoritarian), KJB proactive approach-aggression (coded egalitarian). **Affective grammars are tools detachable from political content** — BC recruits disgust *for* egalitarian ends. Deepens H1: register ⊥ even the show's own politics. Discipline: generative lens, confounds posted, never causal, never near H15/medical history. Warrant: both shows reach for the reactive/proactive-aggression distinction unprompted (KJB scores with it; BC's "incel → martial arts" folk theory).
