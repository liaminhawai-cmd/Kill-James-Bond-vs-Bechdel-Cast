# Synthesis I — Two rhetorical economies, and what n=2 can't decompose

*Skeleton. Essays deposit here while the contribution is fresh; written up last from FINDINGS. Do not wait for all essays to finish.*

## Thesis (provisional)
The two shows run **one analysis through two rhetorical economies**, and the economies covary so tightly with format, nationality, monetisation, and gender-socialisation (the §6 confound table) that the honest claim is not "X causes the difference" but "all the causes point the same way, which is why the pattern is so stable and so hard to decompose."

## Centrepiece exhibit — the live-show natural experiment (essay 03)
The Streatham live KJB show moves *one* confound variable (format: studio trio → present audience) and measures the result:
- **Apologies invert** 11–2 BC-ward → 30–4 KJB, hand-checked as genuine floor-management repair. → repair pragmatics are substantially format-driven.
- **Self-competence deflation appears** in KJB under live conditions ("all my notes were dog shit"; audience out-scoring the hosts on their own SCUM database). → H4's no-self-deflation rule is format-conditioned.
- **What does NOT move:** theory-of-the-listener *intensifies* ("you are the in-group that I bind"; "if you're listening after the fact, fuck off"). → audience-theory is constitutive, not a format accident.
This is the best single piece of evidence that format owns the top rank in the confound table, while carving out what format cannot explain.

The variable is audience, not occasion — proven on both shows. Finer point: live AMPLIFIES BC's native repair register / IMPORTS KJB's otherwise-absent one (H4).
