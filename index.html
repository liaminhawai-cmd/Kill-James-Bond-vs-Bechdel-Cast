About the study

The essays compare films covered by both Kill James Bond! and The Bechdel Cast, working from transcript patterns across lexis, syntax, metaphor, pragmatics and episode structure. They are comparative discourse analysis rather than film reviews or podcast reviews.

Later essays explicitly revise earlier ones. On the site, references to another film or essay link across to that chapter so the argument can be read as a network rather than a straight sequence.
