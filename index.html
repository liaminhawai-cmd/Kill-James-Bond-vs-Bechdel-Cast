Unfinished study · Liam Gomez-Kervin

Kill James Bond!
vs The Bechdel Cast

Comparative discourse analysis of two feminist film podcasts talking about the same films: where their conclusions converge, and how their language, humour, stance and imagined audiences differ.

Work in progress. These are research notes and planned essays, not finished findings.

The project

13 matched films

Each planned essay takes one film covered by both Kill James Bond! and The Bechdel Cast. The film stays constant; the podcasts become the comparison.

The analysis works from transcript patterns across lexis, syntax, metaphor, pragmatics and episode structure. The main question is not which show is “better,” but how two shows with substantially overlapping feminist conclusions build very different rhetorical worlds.

What currently looks interesting

provisional
H3
Affect. BC often polices through disgust, recoil and fatigue; KJB more often through anger, contempt and attack.
H4
Deflation. BC tends to undercut itself; KJB tends to undercut the film, industry or named targets.
H5
Irony. Each show may ironise the thing it is most earnest about: BC its competence, KJB its sincerity.
H7
Attention. KJB often reaches for systems, mechanisms and artifacts; BC more often tracks character, interiority and relationships.
H9
Convergence. On some heist material the substantive feminist analysis nearly merges; much of the difference is rhetorical economy rather than conclusion.
H14
Lore drift. A testable prediction: KJB may become denser and more initiate-facing over time while BC keeps re-onboarding newcomers.
