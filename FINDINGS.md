## Theoretical lenses (added essay 02)

**Schröder / Fight Like an Animal — the affect-grammar reading.** Arnold Schröder's *Fight Like an Animal* corpus (in `/reference/fight_like_an_animal/`, indexed) supplies a biologically-grounded disposition axis we can use as a *generative lens* on H3/H6/H7. Core axis: egalitarian/approach vs authoritarian/avoidance disposition (his "reactivity" trait; McGilchrist's right/left hemispheres; the threat-sensitivity literature). Two distinctions we use:
- **Reactive vs proactive aggression** — reactive = fearful, impulsive, defending a threatened self-image (authoritarian-coded); proactive = sober, threat-detecting, "neither victims nor executioners" (egalitarian-coded). **KJB reaches for this distinction unprompted** when scoring SCUM's violence axis ("it's not reactive violence… it's quite thought out") — which is the warrant for importing the framework at all.
- **Purity-disgust as the affective signature of the authoritarian pole** (Haidt-lineage research Schröder synthesises).

**The affect-grammar inversion (the load-bearing finding):** both shows are egalitarian-left, but their enforcement affect draws on *opposite* poles — BC enforces through purity-disgust (the pole the literature codes authoritarian), KJB through proactive approach-aggression (the pole it codes egalitarian). This is the H3 parallel to the H7 cognitive inversion (AMAB trio = relational topics through systems engine; AFAB duo = systems topics through relational engine). **Discipline:** this does NOT make BC authoritarian. The finding is that *affective grammars are tools detachable from political content* — BC recruits disgust FOR egalitarian ends. That orthogonality deepens H1 (register ⊥ even the show's own politics). NEVER present as causal; confounds §6 (format/nationality/monetisation/gender all aligned) over-determine the affect. Schröder's own refrain — failure comes from "attending to a small subset of salient variables at the expense of complexity" — is posted over the whole reading. **Hard rule:** the lens is used only at the level of affect/cognition grammar; it is NEVER routed near H15 / anyone's medical history.
